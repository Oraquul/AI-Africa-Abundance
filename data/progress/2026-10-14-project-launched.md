  AI Africa Abundance is now a public project.

  The white paper, The Oracle and the Partner (version 3.1, July 2026), argues that abundance is an institutional question. The proposal is that Africa build the oracle layer — benchmarks, data, laboratories, and field networks — rather than train national foundation models.

  The repository tracks 52 proposed oracles, drawn from Annex A and from infrastructure the paper names. None is claimed as built. Leads, dates, and datasets will be added only when contributors supply them, and only with consent where a person is named.

  Read the paper, propose a correction, or help specify an oracle:
  https://oraquul.github.io/AI-Africa-Abundance/

  This is an independent personal initiative of Benedict Itegbe. It is not an official position of the Nigerian Export Promotion Council or any other institution.
