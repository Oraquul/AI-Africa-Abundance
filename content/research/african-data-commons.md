This note is planned. It has not been written.

Section 22 places curated, licensed, documented datasets second, after benchmarks: soils and agronomy, weather, disease surveillance, clinical imaging, electricity telemetry, freight and border movements, satellite and drone archives, language corpora, and materials data. It asks for metadata standards, a licence on each collection, restricted access where the data are sensitive, and collection aimed at gaps rather than passive aggregation. Annex B.2 is the paper’s cost note.

Language corpora are called out there as the sharpest gap. They are tracked separately as an infrastructure oracle.
