This note is planned. It has not been written.

Section 19.1 specifies a continental freight and trade digital twin: roads, rail, ports, and borders with capacity and reliability; commodity flows; cost and time functions checked against observed shipments; and a representation of informal cross-border trade. Section 31 places commissioning that twin in the first two years, as decision support for corridor prioritisation. The tracker records it as proposed.
