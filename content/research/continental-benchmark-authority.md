This note is planned. It has not been written.

Section 22 describes a thinly staffed benchmark authority as the first layer of the build: a published suite of machine-scorable tasks, public leaderboards, held-out test sets, and domain working groups. Its stated function is to protect the integrity of the tests. Annex B.1 sets out the paper’s indicative cost lines for that authority.

Nothing on this page is a funding request or a claim that the authority exists. The [oracle tracker](https://oraquul.github.io/AI-Africa-Abundance/oracles/) is the public list of proposed tasks, not the authority itself.
