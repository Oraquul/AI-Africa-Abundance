This note is planned. It has not been written.

Section 20.1 treats agriculture as a wide loop rather than a fast one: many parallel trials across agro-ecological zones, run with cooperatives, under conditions of consent, compensation, and data rights that the paper calls non-negotiable. It also names tasks inside agriculture that can be scored faster than a season, including image identification, irrigation, and prices. Those tasks are already separate rows in the tracker.
